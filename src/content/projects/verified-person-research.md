## What it does

The skill turns a LinkedIn URL, or a name plus workplace context, into cited professional background research. Quick, Standard, and Deep modes scale the research budget to the request.

## Evidence boundaries

Identity resolution and claim-confidence labels are part of the workflow. Thin evidence produces a shorter result instead of invented connective detail, and contact enrichment, data brokers, and protected-trait inference remain out of scope.
