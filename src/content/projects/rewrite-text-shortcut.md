## How it works

Select a sentence or paragraph and press **⌃⌥⌘R**. Apple Intelligence rewrites it on your Mac. In apps that support macOS Services output, the rewrite replaces the selected text. The shortcut also copies the result to the clipboard so you can paste it yourself.

## Getting started

You’ll need a Mac with Apple Intelligence enabled and the on-device model action available in Shortcuts. Open the downloaded `.shortcut` file to add it to Shortcuts, then follow the setup instructions to enable the Quick Action and check the keyboard shortcut.
