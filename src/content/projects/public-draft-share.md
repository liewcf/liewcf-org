## How to share a draft

Open a draft post or page in the WordPress editor. In the Public Draft Share box, choose how long the link should last. For a week-long review, set **Expires in** to **7 days**. Then choose **Create Link** and send it to your reviewer. They’ll see the draft in your site’s theme, including the layout and images.

If the reviewer finishes early, choose **Disable** in the editor. Publishing the post also turns off its preview link.
