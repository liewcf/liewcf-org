I published the first version of Rewrite Text, a shortcut for tightening English drafts. Select the text you want to rewrite and press **⌃⌥⌘R**. The shortcut uses Apple Intelligence to rewrite the text on your Mac.

The rewrite replaces your selection in supported apps. It’s also copied to the clipboard, so you can paste it yourself if the app leaves the field unchanged.
