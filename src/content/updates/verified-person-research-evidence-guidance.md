The research guidance now requires specific, source-supported circumstances before a claim can be labelled Probable. Repetition across interested-party pages alone is not enough. The revision also downgrades a fictional founding example and removes a degree-date identity exclusion.

The verification record dated 8 September reports that the package validator, Codex metadata validator, and whitespace checks passed. It explicitly says that live behavioral reruns and installed-copy synchronization were not performed. Earlier behavior-test results therefore do not verify these revised instructions.
